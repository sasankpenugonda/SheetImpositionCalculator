Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

The Sticker Imposition Calculator was designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. All mathematical calculations, layout packing, and image processing happen entirely within your web browser (client-side).

2. Your Artwork

When you upload artwork (PNG, JPG) to visualize your layout, that file is never uploaded to our servers. It is read locally by your browser to draw the preview and generate the final PDF. Once you close the tab, the image data is gone.

3. Local Storage

To improve your experience, we use your browser's localStorage to remember your most recent settings (sheet size, margins, gaps). This data remains on your physical device and is never transmitted to us.

4. Analytics

We may use lightweight, privacy-focused analytics (such as Plausible) to understand how many people visit the site and which features are most popular. These tools do not track you across the web or store personally identifiable information.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please visit our Contact page.
